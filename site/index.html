What the county counter means

The frequency map is an observation-based screening analysis, not an NWS gridded analysis. For each calendar day from September through May, the viewer takes the coldest selected IEM station value assigned to each county. If no station exists inside a county, the nearest regional station is used and the county popup identifies that fallback.

The custom slider and Frost/Freeze/Hard Freeze presets use minimum air temperature. Cold Weather Advisory and Extreme Cold presets use the colder of daily minimum air temperature and IEM minimum “feels like” value.

Season-product verification

  • Frost Advisory: temperature-support screen at ≤36°F; frost itself is not directly observed by ASOS/AWOS.
  • Freeze Watch/Warning: observed minimum temperature ≤32°F.
  • Cold Weather Advisory: local county apparent/air-temperature advisory threshold.
  • Extreme Cold Watch/Warning: local county warning threshold.
  • Legacy Wind Chill and Hard Freeze products are plotted for older seasons; verification is labeled as a proxy because historical local criteria can differ.

Data sources

  • Iowa Environmental Mesonet computed daily summaries (`min_temp_f`, `min_feel`).
  • IEM VTEC archive for FR/FZ/CW/EC plus legacy WC/HZ products.
  • NOAA/NWS reference county boundaries and CWA assignments.
